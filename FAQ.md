# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Account & Billing

### Q1: How do I update my billing address?
**A:** You can update your billing address from your account settings:
1. Log in to your account
2. Go to Settings → Billing & Payments
3. Click "Edit Address"
4. Update your address information and click "Save"

Changes take effect within 24 hours and will apply to future invoices.

### Q2: What payment methods do you accept?
**A:** We accept the following payment methods:
- Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- Bank Transfer (ACH in the US)
- PayPal
- Wire Transfer (for enterprise customers)

All payments are processed securely using industry-standard encryption.

### Q3: When am I billed?
**A:** Billing occurs on the same day each month as your subscription start date. For example, if you signed up on the 15th, you'll be billed on the 15th of each month. Annual subscriptions are billed once per year.

### Q4: How do I cancel my subscription?
**A:** To cancel your subscription:
1. Log in to your account
2. Go to Settings → Subscription & Plans
3. Click "Cancel Subscription"
4. Select a reason (optional)
5. Confirm cancellation

Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period. No refunds are issued for partial months.

### Q5: Do you offer refunds?
**A:** Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase for annual plans if you haven't used more than 10% of your subscription features. Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable per our terms of service. For refund requests, contact our billing team.

## Product Features & Setup

### Q6: How do I get started with the platform?
**A:** Getting started is easy:
1. Create an account on our website
2. Complete the setup wizard (usually takes 5-10 minutes)
3. Configure your API keys if using our API
4. Choose your subscription plan
5. Start using your selected features

Detailed setup guides are available in our Help Center.

### Q7: What is your free trial?
**A:** We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card is required to start the trial. After 14 days, you must choose a paid plan to continue using the service.

### Q8: Is there an API available?
**A:** Yes! We provide a comprehensive REST API and GraphQL endpoint. API access is included with all paid plans. Documentation is available at `/api/docs` and sample code in multiple languages is available on our GitHub.

### Q9: What are the system requirements?
**A:** Our platform is cloud-based and works on:
- All modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Desktop and mobile devices
- Minimum recommended: 1 GB RAM, stable internet connection

No installation required.

### Q10: Can I export my data?
**A:** Yes, you can export your data anytime:
1. Go to Settings → Data & Privacy
2. Click "Export Data"
3. Choose format (CSV, JSON, XML)
4. Exports are generated within 24 hours and sent to your email

Data exports are available for all plan types.

## Technical Support

### Q11: What are your service hours?
**A:** We provide 24/7 platform access. Technical support is available:
- **Free Plans:** Email support (48-hour response time)
- **Pro Plans:** Email & Chat (24-hour response time)
- **Enterprise Plans:** 24/7 phone, email, and chat support with dedicated account manager

### Q12: How reliable is your service?
**A:** We maintain a 99.9% uptime SLA (Service Level Agreement). Our infrastructure is distributed across multiple availability zones to ensure redundancy and reliability. Status updates are published at status.company.com.

### Q13: Is my data secure?
**A:** Security is our top priority:
- All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- We undergo annual SOC 2 Type II audits
- We comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations
- Regular security testing and vulnerability assessments are performed
- No third-party access to your data without your consent

### Q14: How do I reset my password?
**A:** To reset your password:
1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Click the link in the confirmation email
4. Create a new password
5. Log in with your new password

Password reset links expire after 24 hours.

### Q15: Do you provide integrations with other tools?
**A:** Yes, we offer pre-built integrations with:
- Slack, Microsoft Teams
- Zapier, IFTTT
- Salesforce, HubSpot
- Google Workspace, Microsoft 365
- Custom webhook support

Check our Integrations Marketplace for the full list and setup instructions.

## Policies & Legal

### Q16: What is your refund policy?
**A:** Our refund policy:
- **Annual Plans:** 30-day money-back guarantee if unused (less than 10% feature utilization)
- **Monthly Plans:** No refunds; you can cancel anytime
- Refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of purchase
- Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days

### Q17: What happens to my data if I delete my account?
**A:** When you delete your account:
- All your data is permanently deleted within 30 days
- You cannot recover deleted data
- Backups are retained for 90 days per compliance requirements, then destroyed
- A confirmation email is sent before deletion begins

### Q18: Do you comply with GDPR?
**A:** Yes, we are fully GDPR compliant:
- Users can request data access, correction, or deletion
- We have a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) for EU customers
- Privacy Policy is available on our website
- No data is transferred outside the EU for EU-based accounts

### Q19: How often do you update your service?
**A:** We deploy updates continuously with a release cycle of:
- **Major updates:** Quarterly
- **Feature releases:** Monthly
- **Security patches:** As needed (typically weekly)
- **Bug fixes:** Ongoing

Updates are deployed during low-traffic periods (2-4 AM UTC) with zero downtime where possible.

### Q20: Can I import data from other platforms?
**A:** Yes, we support imports from most competing platforms:
- CSV/Excel file uploads
- Direct database connections
- API-to-API data migration
- Custom scripts for bulk operations

Contact our onboarding team for assistance with large migrations.
